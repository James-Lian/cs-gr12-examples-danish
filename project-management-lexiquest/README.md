### How to Play
Arrow keys to move around the maze. You move around the maze to collect letters. You can view the letters you've collected by clicking on the item bag icon.  
Press Q to view Wordnik API/Rita in action.

You use the letters you've collected to answer riddles, which you can view by clicking on the item bag. In the item bag/riddle menu, "type" out your answer to use the letters you've collected to spell out word answers to the riddles.

If you need help finding a specific letter, select a letter from the dropdown in the bottom-left corner created by P5.js, and then click the checkmark button. This will conduct a recursive breadth-first search that will lead you to the nearest available letter of your choosing.
